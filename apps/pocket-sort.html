Pocket Sort

Android Volume Live

Rockets, books, gems and plants. Four to a tray, and every tray one kind.

Pocket Sort turns rockets, books, bottles, gems, cameras and plants into sorting puzzles. You choose a tray and move its highlighted collectible onto a matching item or into an empty space. Each tray holds four, and a collection is complete when every item of one kind sits together in a full tray. The rules are short. Finding room for the next move is where the planning comes in. A journey of collections runs through six named rooms, and a separate daily collection waits each day. Every collectible can be told apart by shape as well as color. Tap or drag to move items, and use free hints, undo or a restart when you get stuck. Leave whenever you like and your current board and hint inventory are kept. Core puzzles work offline, with no account and no purchases. Optional rewarded videos give extra hints, and occasional interstitial ads show between finished rounds.
