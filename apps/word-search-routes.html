Word Search Routes

Android Volume Store prep

Eight words per grid, twelve themes, and a new route through the letters every day.

Word Search Routes turns each letter grid into a small expedition. Every route hides eight words, and you find them along straight lines across, down or diagonally, in either direction. Drag through the letters, tap the first and last letter, or use a keyboard. Each word you find leaves a colorful trail across the map. There are 12 themes, from gardens and the coast to music, the night sky and the kitchen, with 96 chosen words in each language. Switching language in Settings changes both the interface and the word lists, in English, Spanish or Brazilian Portuguese, and accented words appear exactly as they are written. Follow the journey of puzzles or come back for the daily route. Each route starts with three free clues, and revealed first letters stay marked while you search. There is no countdown, and your finds and clues are saved if you leave. Core play works offline, with no account and no in-app purchases. Optional rewarded videos give extra help.
