Office Life

Android Volume Live

Twelve weeks of probation at a startup. Your boss, your rival and the founder all text.

Day one at a forty-person startup, and the whole game is your phone. Your manager wants weekly updates and means it. Your work bestie has already sent you the group chat's history. Your rival replies-all. People Ops keeps a record of everything. The founder texts at 2am and expects an answer. All five have your number, all five read #general, and you have twelve weeks until the probation review. Messages land week by week and you pick every reply. No typing, no timers. Everyone remembers what you said, so backing one person costs you with the rest, and if the heat climbs, #general leaks it to the whole company. Twelve chapters, over 800 messages, twelve endings from Promoted to Extended to The 2am Job, and sixty moments to collect. Read Their Mind lets you watch a short ad to preview what a reply will do. The company and its people are invented. English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Free with ads, no account, plays offline.
