Mic Drop

Android, PC Volume Live

Rap battle meets deckbuilding. Chain rhymes, drop bars, save the station.

Every card is a bar and every turn is a verse. Dez Vega washes dishes and writes rhymes on order tickets, and a media giant is about to buy the community radio station that raised the neighborhood. The only way to stop it is to win the Golden Mic. Fill four beat slots, chain cards from the same rhyme family to multiply Hype, tap on the beat for flow, and break your rival before the crowd turns.
