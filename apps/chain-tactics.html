Chain Tactics

PC Volume Live

Every move carries a letter home.

Travel with Lova through five coastal chapters, delivering one letter at a time. Fanorona is the Malagasy capture game: each side starts with 22 pieces on a 9×5 board and slides along the lines, capturing the row ahead by approach or the row behind by withdrawal, then chaining more captures with the same piece. If a capture is available, you must take it. A 35-delivery courier route comes with a painted postcard album, along with 80 capture puzzles and a daily challenge, against the computer on three levels or a second player.
