Market Morning Mystery

PC Volume Live

Find every lost delivery before the market opens.

You are the new warden of Puddingstone Market. Every dawn the deliveries arrive mixed up, cheese at the bakery, carrots on a café table, a cherry pie on the waffle stand, and you sort it out before the opening bell. Look over a little 3D market square from above, move around and zoom in, then find the one item that is out of place. Over forty mornings the deliveries keep going wrong in the same odd way. Meet the vendors, follow crumbs and a blue ribbon, and find out who has been moving the bread, and why.
