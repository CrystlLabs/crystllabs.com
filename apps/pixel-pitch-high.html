Pixel Pitch High

Android Volume Live

One school year, a 3v3 match every Friday, and a scout who is not counting goals.

You transfer to Eastside High with a reputation you did not choose and one school year to replace it. Pixel Pitch High is a soccer career story. Each week is five days of three slots: class in the morning, an afternoon for training, a diner shift, the library, the physio or whoever asked first, and an evening that goes to whoever you give it to. Friday is the fixture, and you play it yourself: compact 3v3 with a virtual stick, sprint, through balls, shot power and placement and teammate commands. Coach Reyes sets an objective before kickoff and judges it whatever the score. Energy, health, grades and eligibility all count, and rumors spread if you ignore them. Scout Marcus Vale weighs teamwork, discipline, academics, composure and consistency far above goals, and his report shows its arithmetic. Five acts, and an ending that depends on who you kept. Offline, no account, opt-in rewarded ads, nothing for sale.
