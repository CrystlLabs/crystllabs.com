Sugar Sort

PC Volume Live

Find three matching treats, mind your seven-space tray, and fill a dessert collection.

Sugar Sort is a triple-match puzzle full of pixel-art desserts. Pick uncovered treats into your seven-space tray, and three of a kind clear together. Look beneath the top layers, plan around the spaces you have left and keep a sweet streak going. Trays change shape as you go, from full trays to open centers, narrow middles and staggered layers, and every one starts with a possible clearing path. Play relaxed with no countdown, try timed trays or take the seeded daily tasting, and discover 30 desserts with collection stars and a display for six favorites.
