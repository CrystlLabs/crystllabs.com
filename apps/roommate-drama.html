Roommate Drama

Android Volume Live

One flat, five people, twelve months of texts. Everyone remembers what you said.

Roommate Drama is a texting game played entirely in your phone's messages. You just signed a twelve-month lease. Sam is warm, chaotic and has never once paid rent on the day. Dee is precise, quiet and laminates the chore chart. The landlord texts in one-sentence messages that end with a full stop, Sam's partner does not live here but has a key, and the neighbour downstairs hears everything and bakes before she complains. You read the messages as they land, month by month, and pick every reply. No typing and no timers. Everyone remembers what you said, and siding with one costs you with the rest. Let the drama climb and the group chat leaks it to everyone. There are twelve chapters, nearly a thousand messages, twelve endings and sixty moments to collect. It is a comedy about living with people. Free with ads, no account, nothing to buy, and it plays offline once installed.
