Hide & Gnome

Android Volume Store prep

Hidden object hunts in tiny 3D towns. Find your list, then spot the gnomes.

Hide & Gnome is a hidden object game set in tiny 3D towns. Drag to look around, pinch to zoom, and tap each thing on your list: three cones, two buckets, a mailbox. Every find flies into the tray at the bottom. Somewhere in each level, one to three garden gnomes are hiding too, and each one you spot joins your garden. There are 120 levels across 12 places, from the park and the market to the harbour, the roadworks and a rooftop. Scenes grow from 60 props with 6 things to find up to 220 props with 12. There is no timer on screen, but finding everything fast earns three stars. Tap with care: three wrong taps in two seconds locks tapping for three seconds. Every item can be seen from some angle and has no look-alike decor in its level. Coins buy 24 garden pieces. A new daily level, hints and a radar. Plays offline, no account, free with ads between levels. No purchases.
