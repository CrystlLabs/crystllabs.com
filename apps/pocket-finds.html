Pocket Finds

Android Volume Live

Six illustrated scenes, small details to find, no timer. Zoom in and take your time.

A sleepy cat in the greenhouse. A compass by the harbor. A little telescope under the stars. Pocket Finds is a hidden object game about slowing down and looking closely. You explore six original illustrated scenes, a morning greenhouse, a harbor, a cottage kitchen, a maker's workshop, a quiet bookshop and a rooftop observatory, with more than 60 distinct objects between them. Each collection asks you to find 8 to 11 pictured objects. As your journey grows you return to familiar scenes with new collections and the objects moved around. Pinch, use the zoom buttons or scroll to inspect details, drag to explore, and choose Fit to see the whole scene. You get three free clues per collection, and an active clue can be shown again without spending another. There is a separate daily collection, and your finds, clues and camera position are saved when you leave. No timer. Core play works offline, with no account and no purchases. Free with ads at round breaks.
