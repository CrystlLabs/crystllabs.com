The Last Bell

PC Volume Live

Train, box and build a legacy in a career where every contract has a cost.

An aging club fighter gets one final title route through a promoter who owns every opponent's contract. Rebuild the gym and decide what your name will mean. Choose a bout contract from four to twelve rounds, then use jabs, crosses, hooks, body shots, defensive movement and clinches while judging distance, timing and stamina. Make corner adjustments and fight through knockdowns, stoppages or scorecards. Away from the match, work through 36 story encounters with 12 recurring characters, where choices shape trust, debt, community support and relationships, and earlier decisions come back later. Balance training, recovery, sponsors and club improvements across a 12-fixture season, with three career backgrounds and retirement legacies.
