Theme Studio

Android Volume Store prep

Original wallpapers, matching color palettes and a home-screen date widget.

Theme Studio puts original wallpaper collections, coordinated color palettes and a simple date widget in one place. You browse six art packs, preview a wallpaper, and find colors that go with it. Each design comes in light and dark treatments. When you find one you like, save it or apply it as your Android wallpaper, then add the native date widget to your home screen. There are 36 designs across the six collections. Twenty-four are available from the start with no video unlock, and the other 12 can be unlocked on your device with optional rewarded videos. Favorites are saved, and the app returns to your last design and light or dark setting. Core browsing and the included wallpaper tools work offline, with no account and no purchases. Occasional interstitial ads show at breaks between tasks. Wallpaper and widget placement options depend on your device and launcher. Available in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
