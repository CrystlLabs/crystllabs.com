Pace & Promises

PC Volume Live

Find your rhythm, join the running club and see where friendship leads.

There is a place for you in the Sunday Striders. Run through painted city routes, find a comfortable pace and get to know six adults whose lives keep crossing yours at the café. A ten-week season brings 30 rhythm runs on eight illustrated routes. Step on the beat or turn on Assisted pace, since both earn the full reward, and choose your effort at two bends each run. Between runs, share 36 café conversations with six runners. The story spans six chapters, with six relationship endings where you choose friendship or romance and three season endings.
