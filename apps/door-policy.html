Door Policy

Android Volume Live

Bouncer on a basement door. Decide who gets in. Find out in the morning.

You are the bouncer on the door of a basement club. A card in your pocket lists tonight's rules, the queue comes up the steps one at a time, and you decide who gets in. In the morning you find out what you caused. Tap NO or IN to judge somebody, or swipe to move quickly, and tap a guest's card to look at the ID properly. The shutter comes down at three, then the morning pan shows the room you made, the takings, and a note from the owner. About three minutes a night, sixty nights on the calendar. The card changes every night, so last night's habits are not tonight's answer. 60,291 distinct faces, and a forged ID always leaves something visible.
