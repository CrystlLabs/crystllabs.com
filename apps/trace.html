Trace

Android Volume Live

Crime scene cleaner, one room a night. Make it boring before six in the morning.

A client texts you an address and a deadline, and never says what happened. This is a crime scene cleaner game about one room a night. You have until six in the morning to make the room boring. The text arrives, the room comes up all at once, and you go through it. Six to twelve traces are in there at three tiers of visibility: a stain, a scuff, a smear, a burn, a shard, a footprint. The obvious ones are free. The rest are under, behind and inside things, so drag anything that will move and open anything with a lid. A room scores on boring, not clean. Leaving early with everything found pays more. Nothing is shown: no wound, no weapon, no body.
