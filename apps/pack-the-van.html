Pack the Van

Android Volume Store prep

The heist went fine. Now fit the loot in the van before the sirens arrive.

Pack the Van is a packing puzzle with a heist on the clock. The job went fine. Now everything on the rug has to fit in the back of the van before the sirens arrive, and some of it breaks if you stack the wrong thing on top. Tap a piece of loot to pick it, tap Rotate to turn it, and drag it into the van. A green ghost shows where it fits and a red one shows where it will not. Heavy things crush fragile things: a safe on a jewellery box is a very expensive mistake. Fill the van to the target value and shut the doors. There are 50 vans, from 8 pieces and 75 seconds up to 14 pieces and 45 seconds, and every van is built packed first and then emptied, so everything can always fit. Thirty kinds of loot, three stars per van, and a lock-up to fill with 40 trophies. Free with optional rewarded ads. No purchases, no account.
