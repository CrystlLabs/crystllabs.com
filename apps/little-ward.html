Little Ward

Android Volume Store prep

Lay out a small clinic, shorten the queues, and keep the staff from burning out.

Little Ward is a clinic management sim. You lay out a welcoming neighborhood clinic, guide patients through their visits and support the staff who keep it running. Arrange reception, consultation rooms, labs, imaging and observation areas, then handle supplies, cleaning, training and staff fatigue. Patients arrive with 24 fictional case types, each with its own required stages, and you improve the flow by choosing where rooms go and which queues get priority. A room in the right place or a shorter queue makes a visible difference. The clinic grows through 60 management contracts. You can pause when you need to think and change the simulation speed as things get busier, and three save slots let you try different approaches. Core play and progression work offline, in English, Spanish or Brazilian Portuguese. Free, with optional rewarded ads for a bonus and ads at breaks between shifts. Little Ward is fiction and not a source of medical advice.
