Clean Getaway

PC Volume Live

Draw a route, time patrols and recover masterpieces in a 3D heist puzzle.

Art restorer Iris Calloway discovers that Victor Crane is selling the museum's originals and replacing them with her practice copies. With locksmith Ozzie and hacker Juno, she sets out to bring them home. Draw a route across a 3D floor plan, add waits, lockpicks, camera hacks, thrown coins and hiding spots, and scrub the timeline to preview patrols. Then press GO and watch Iris follow your plan, where half a second in sight trips the alarm. 60 jobs across six heists lead to a penthouse finale, with a gallery of 61 real Open Access paintings.
