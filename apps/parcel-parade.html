Parcel Parade

Android Volume Live

Route parcels across an island chain and find out why the island mail stopped.

Mara inherits the last little depot on a disconnected island chain. A parcel addressed to her missing grandfather becomes the first clue in a trail of undelivered letters. Reopen one route at a time and discover why the island mail stopped. Solve 80 routing puzzles across five story chapters using switches, timed buffers and a large-parcel splitter, with departure windows, express deliveries and route previews. Collect six decorative finishes, return for seeded daily and weekly puzzles, and build your own variation in the challenge studio to share as a text code.
