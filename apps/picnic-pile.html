Picnic Pile

Android Volume Store prep

Pick food off a 3D picnic pile, match three alike in the tray, and clear the blanket.

Picnic Pile is a triple match puzzle on a picnic blanket buried in 3D food. Tap an item on top of the pile to send it to the tray, and match three alike to clear them. The tray holds seven, so you plan your picks. Every tap takes the item right where you touched, and pulling one out from under others makes the food it was holding up tumble into new places. Fill the tray without a match or run out of time and the level is lost. Piles start at 30 items and grow to 90 in 26 kinds, drawn from 40 foods like burgers, tacos, watermelons and donuts. Clear inside half the timer for three stars, and pop the two kinds on each level's order card first for bonus coins. There is a daily pile, and coins buy 4 blankets and 20 pieces for your picnic spot. Plays offline with no account. Free, with ads between levels, never during play, and no purchases.
