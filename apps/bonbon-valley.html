Bonbon Valley

PC Volume Live

Swap and blast candies across 300 levels to win back a sugar-frosted valley.

The Sour Baron has bottled up the valley's sweetness, and Lulu and her grandpa run the last candy shop left. Ride the candy cart from Lollipop Meadow to the Gloom Factory across 300 levels. Match four for a striped candy, an L or T for a wrapped one, five for a Rainbow Drop, and swap two specials together for screen-clearing combos.
