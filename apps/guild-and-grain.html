Guild & Grain

Android Volume Store prep

Run the village that feeds, equips and sends off the adventuring party.

Guild & Grain is a fantasy village sim where the adventure starts at home. Before a party heads out it needs a warm meal, sharp tools and a village worth coming back to, and building that is your job. Farms, sawmills and mines feed crafting across 25 facility types, so you plan production before you commit to an expedition. Thirty recipes turn raw goods into useful equipment. You recruit 12 specialists, improve how well your adventurers are prepared, and balance village morale, supplies and trade against what each expedition needs. Sixty contracts and a continuing adventure loop keep the settlement growing. Pause whenever you need to think, change the simulation speed as things grow, and try different plans in three save slots. Core play and progression work offline. Optional rewarded ads give a bonus, and interstitial ads appear at breaks between shifts. Plays in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
