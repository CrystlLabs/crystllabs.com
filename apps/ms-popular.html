Ms. Popular

Android Volume Live

A texting dating sim. Five men, one channel, and a phone that never stops buzzing.

One video did ten million views, and now everybody wants a piece of you. In Ms. Popular you are a beauty creator with 1.2 million subscribers, a manager, an editor who has been there since the ring light sat on a stack of books, and five men in your phone who all want something. You never walk anywhere and never type. You read, pick what to say, and the people on the other end remember it. A season runs twelve chapters, each ending in a decision you cannot take back. You manage Reach, Craft and Heat: the choice that grows the channel usually costs the work, and the one that protects the work usually costs money. Keeping every man warm costs trust with the others. There are twelve endings, all reachable without spending anything. Optional ads let you preview a reply, skip a chapter wait or bank Charm. No purchases. English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
