Heel Turn

Android Volume Live

Read the crowd, work the match, or steal the finish in a wrestling career RPG.

The finish is written. Your reputation isn't. Heel Turn is a choice-driven wrestling game about eight shows, one main event, and every dangerous decision between the bells. Read your rival's tells, manage your stamina, work the crowd, and choose whether to honor the booked finish or go into business for yourself. Work clean sequences to earn locker-room trust. Make it real to hurt your rival and draw a louder reaction. Never throw a wild shot when your opponent is braced to counter. Between shows, spend your purse on recovery, rehearse to build permanent ringcraft, take safer bookings or demand the spotlight. Promoters remember whether you delivered. The locker room remembers even more.
