Tiffin Trail

PC Volume Live

A little lunch. A thoughtful route.

Every lunch has someone waiting for it. Join a small delivery cooperative that carries home-cooked tiffins from family kitchens to neighbours across a miniature town. Plan the courier's trip from depot 0: pick up every lunch, deliver it on time and get back to the depot, minding the carrying space, delivery windows and any delivery that must happen first. Then send the courier and watch the plan play out. There are 60 routes in four districts, where road closures, tight windows and full bags make later routes real puzzles, plus a daily route and a relaxed mode with no delivery windows.
