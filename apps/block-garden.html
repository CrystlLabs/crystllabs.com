Block Garden

Android Volume Live

An 8 by 8 block puzzle where your score grows a garden. The pieces never rotate.

Block Garden is an 8 by 8 block puzzle. You get a tray of three pieces, place them on the board, and any row or column you fill clears. Clear several lines at once, rows and columns together, and the score climbs faster. Pieces keep their orientation, so you plan around the shapes you are given instead of turning them to fit. As your score passes milestones, flowers bloom in the garden around the board. Endless mode runs for as long as there is room left. The daily goal asks for 1,500 points within 30 placements. You can undo recent moves for free, and once per round you can prune a 2 by 2 patch to open up space. Leave mid-game and the board, tray and score are waiting when you come back. Core play works offline, with no account and no purchases. Optional rewarded videos give extra prunes, and occasional interstitial ads show between finished rounds.
