Color Recall

Android Volume Live

Name a famous thing, mix its color from memory, see how far off you really were.

You think you know what color a ripe banana is. Or a stop sign, a tennis ball, the sky at noon. Color Recall asks you to mix it from memory with nothing to look at. You get a name and an orb. Turn the orb until the color looks right, lock it in, and the real color appears next to yours with a score. Scoring follows how the eye sees difference, not just raw numbers, and most people are further off than they expect. There are ten prompts a day, the same ten for everyone, drawn from nearly 300 things: fruit, flags, animals, weather, sports gear and famous shades. When the daily is done there is free play, and you can catch up on yesterday's ten. Two hints help when you are lost: Warmer or Colder, and a Second Swatch. Streaks and a spoiler-free share grid. No account, no typing, nothing to buy. Plays offline once installed. The game is in English.
