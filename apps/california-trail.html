California Trail

Android Volume Early dev

Oregon Trail, except the frontier is the 405 and the oxen are tires.

The rent notice arrives and the number is impossible. A family loads everything it owns into a rusted minivan and drives eight hundred miles south from Humboldt County to a cousin's spare room in San Diego. California Trail plays The Oregon Trail straight, beat for beat, with the freeway as the frontier and the 405 at four in the afternoon as the river you have to ford. Affectionate parody with real teeth: the warmth is aimed at the family, the bite at rent, water policy, wildfire season and gig work. The family is never the joke.
