Junction Stories

Android Volume Store prep

Lay the track, time the trains, and hear what each town has to say once it is connected.

Junction Stories is a railway sim about the places at either end of the line. You lay track, turning curves and junctions until their openings meet the neighboring rails, connect stations, and use signals and passing junctions so trains can share the network. Twelve rolling-stock configurations cover passenger and freight work. You balance the two services against each other, set fares and departure intervals, and keep an eye on wear. As the line spreads across five regions, from Pinehill Branch to the Regional Network, 40 town stories open up. Sixty railway contracts set the goals along the way. Pause whenever you need to think, change the simulation speed as things grow, and try different plans in three save slots. Core play and progression work offline. Optional rewarded ads give a bonus, and interstitial ads appear at breaks between shifts. Plays in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
