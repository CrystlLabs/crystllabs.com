Clean Beans

Android Volume Store prep

Coffee out front, washers out back. Nothing dirty in sight when the inspector walks in.

Clean Beans is a cozy cafe tycoon with a secret. Out front you serve coffee to regulars. Out back the washers are running, and it is not laundry. An inspector drops in on a timer, and while he is inside there can be nothing dirty in your hands or on the pile. You drag a joystick to walk and stand next to things to use them. Carry cups from the coffee machine to the tables for honest money. Carry the delivery boxes to the washers, wait out the cycle, and get the clean stacks into the safe before he arrives. Upgrade pads add tables, a fifth washer and bigger hands. Hired baristas serve on their own, the cafe keeps earning while you are away for up to two hours, and 40 pieces of furniture appear in the shop as you buy them. Free with optional rewarded ads. No purchases, no account.
