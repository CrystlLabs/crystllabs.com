Murder Jump

Android Volume Live

Climb the rooftops, chase the lead, then name the culprit, motive, method and proof.

Murder Jump is a noir detective game set above the streets of Greyglass. You play Pip, a rooftop detective who retraces witnesses' routes through elevators, stations, galleries, radio towers, motels and courthouse heights. Each investigation runs through three routes: a climb, a chase across the rooftops and a descent through hidden service paths, with checkpoints and route help after repeated falls. Recovered clues go onto a case board, where you connect documents, photos, recordings and physical traces, rebuild timelines, challenge testimony and dismiss red herrings. To close a case you name the culprit, the motive, the method and the decisive evidence. A wrong or partial theory sends you back to the evidence instead of ending the case. A short training case teaches the basics, then eight original mysteries open up in the order you choose with Case Keys. Twenty-four fragments across them lead to a missing inspector and Case 17. Unlocked cases play offline. Ads are optional.
