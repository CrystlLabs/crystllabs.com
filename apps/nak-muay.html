Nak Muay

PC Volume Live

Master eight limbs, train at camp and fight from Isan to Bangkok. Play offline.

Lek takes her injured brother's gloves to Kru Somchai's camp. Ten story fights lead from village temple fairs to Bangkok's grand stadium, where champion Dao awaits. Read the tell, check kicks, battle for the clinch and land punches, elbows, knees and teeps in real-time fights won by knockout or judges' decision. Choose one of three starting styles, learn forty techniques and face thirty fictional opponents across four tiers. Between bouts, run, work pads, spar or rest at camp, and upgrade the ring, bags, pads and kitchen.
