Blacktide Salvage

PC Volume Live

Dive into forgotten wrecks. Take the salvage. Make it back alive.

Your salvage union buys an obsolete submarine to search the wreck that took your captain. Fit three modules at the dock, steer through the wreck, ping the sonar to find salvage and hold the cutter on a marker to bring it aboard, then reach the surface buoy before your air runs out, knowing a full hold rises slowly. Each dive turns up another piece of an industrial cover-up. There are 16 wrecks across 4 depth zones, 24 equipment modules in six families and 12 captain's logs leading to a final choice about the archive, plus a story campaign, a daily challenge and an endless mode.
