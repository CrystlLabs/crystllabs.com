Mancala Grove

Android Volume Store prep

Kalah, Oware and Congkak on a wooden board. See where every stone lands first.

Mancala Grove puts one of the oldest board games in the world on a wooden board. Sow stones around the pits, capture your opponent's and fill your store. Three classic rule sets are included: Kalah, Oware (Abapa) and a six-pit version of Congkak. Hold a pit and rings light up every pit your stones will land in, so you can plan before you commit, then let go to sow. Climb a ladder of 40 matches against the computer, which gets stronger as you go, with two seed puzzles between matches. All 80 puzzles are checked by a solver. You can also play two players on one phone, or try the daily puzzle. Earn up to three stars per level by winning by a wide margin or solving at the first try. Coins buy 3 wooden boards and 5 stone sets, and 2x speed is there when you want a quicker game. Plays offline, no account needed. Free with ads between levels, never during play. No purchases.
