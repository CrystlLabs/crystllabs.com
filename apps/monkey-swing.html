Monkey Swing

Android Volume Store prep

Hold to grab a vine, let go to fly. See how far one cube monkey can swing.

Monkey Swing is a one-finger swinging game for kids and grown-ups. A little cube monkey is flying through an endless jungle. Hold your finger down and a vine shoots out to the nearest peg, flower or branch above him, marked by a white ring. He swings on it like a real pendulum. Let go on the upswing and he flies off with all the speed he built up. Let go too early and he flops, hang on too long and he swings back, and if he misses the next vine he drops into the bushes. Your score is the distance in metres, and the gaps get wider the further you go. Red mushrooms bounce you out of a bad jump, and bunches of bananas earn coins. Spend them on nine chunky cube pets: monkey, koala, cat, fox, bunny, panda, chick, tiger and parrot. They all swing the same, so pick your favourite. Free with ads, nothing to buy, no account, plays offline.
