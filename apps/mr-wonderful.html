Mr. Wonderful

Android Volume Live

A rookie pro, five women, one tabloid and a phone that will not stop buzzing.

Mr. Wonderful is a texting dating game. You are a rookie on a pro basketball team, and your phone will not stop. Five women want your attention: Nova, the beat reporter on your own team; Priya, the team physio; Jade, a pop star who posts things; Camille, a sports lawyer and your agent's daughter; and Ruby, the one from home. A tabloid account called THE FEED wants a story, and your coach wants your head in the game. A season runs twelve chapters, and each ends with a decision you cannot take back. You never type. You pick every reply, and people remember it. Keeping everyone warm costs trust, so chasing all five loses. You also manage Clout, Focus and Heat, and growing your name usually costs your game. There are twelve endings, all reachable without paying, in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Free with optional ads, no purchases.
