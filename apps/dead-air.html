Dead Air

PC Volume Live

Start a true crime podcast in your garage, grow a studio and solve cold cases.

Your garage, two microphones and a cold case nobody ever solved. Dead Air is a true crime podcast tycoon: record episodes, grow your audience, hire staff and turn a garage studio into a six-floor media building. Then spend what you earn on cold cases, one season at a time. Each season has eight leads, from interviews to records requests. Finish six, then name your suspect live on air: get it right for a record audience, or get it wrong and get sued. Six seasons run from a 1998 disappearance to a caller who knows too much, with endless bonus cases and a daily case.
