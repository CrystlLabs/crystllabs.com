Rich Man Poor Man

Android Volume Live

One deck a day. Rich thing, poor thing, or both? Three minutes, and harder than it looks.

Rich Man Poor Man is a daily quiz with one new deck every day. Each card names something people do, own, say or eat, and you decide whether it is a rich thing, a poor thing, or both. Then the answer lands with a line that explains why, and the next card slides in. A deck takes about three minutes. Everyone gets the same cards on the same day, so you can compare results with friends, and a share card shows your result as an emoji grid with no spoilers. The game draws on 655 scenarios written for it. When the daily is done you can keep going in free play, or catch up on yesterday's deck if you missed it. A streak grows with each day you play. Stuck on a card, you can watch a short ad for a Second Opinion hint. Free with ads, no account, no typing, nothing to buy, and it plays offline once installed. The game is in English.
