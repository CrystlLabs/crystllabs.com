Water Polo Smash

Android Flagship Live

Drag back, load the spin, and bend the ball past the keeper.

Water Polo Smash is a shooting game with one control: drag back, aim, let go. The spin you put on the throw is the whole game — bend it around the keeper's hand and it drops into the top corner, get greedy and it comes off the post. Simulation mode runs real air drag and gravity, so clean technique scores. Arcade throws that out for wild curves and combo multipliers. Boss keepers every fifth level, a trophy room to fill, and a Trajectory Lab that exposes the physics so you can tune your curve before you take it into a match.
