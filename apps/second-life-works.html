Second Life Works

Android Volume Store prep

Turn a pile of scrap into useful products with conveyors, sorters and a lot of planning.

Second Life Works is a recycling factory sim. You start with a pile of scrap and build a workshop that turns it into finished products through a production line you design. There are 28 machine and facility types, and you route materials through conveyors, splitters, sorters and processors to make 40 items, from small fittings to larger household goods. The line does not run itself. Contamination creeps in, power runs short, machines wear down and buffers back up, and keeping all of that under control is the job. Save your layout, try challenge variants and work through 60 factory contracts. You can pause when you need to think, change the simulation speed as the operation grows, and keep three save slots for different approaches. Core play and progression work offline. Available in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Free, with optional rewarded ads for a bonus and ads at breaks between shifts.
