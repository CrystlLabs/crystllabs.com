Cable Tangle

Android Volume Live

Drag the plugs until no two cables cross. Five plugs to start, twelve by the end.

Cable Tangle is a logic puzzle about making room. Each circuit is a knot of numbered plugs joined by cables, and you drag the plugs around until no cable crosses another. Glowing × markers show every crossing, and red rings warn you when a plug needs more space or a cable runs through it. The journey starts with five plugs and works up to twelve-plug networks, with a separate daily circuit on top. Undo is free, restart is always there, and each circuit comes with three free guides that suggest plug positions. If you leave mid-puzzle, your arrangement, move count and undo history are saved. There is no timer, so you think at your own pace. Plays offline with no account and no in-app purchases. Optional rewarded videos give extra help, and occasional ads show between finished rounds.
