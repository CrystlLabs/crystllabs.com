Canopy Disc Golf

Android Volume Store prep

Read the breeze, set the power, and curve a disc through the trees into the basket.

Canopy Disc Golf is disc golf in the woods. On each throw you read the wind, set your power and pick how the disc curves, then watch it thread the trees toward the basket. There are 54 holes across six woodland settings, 18 disc profiles to learn and 36 skill challenges. Optional flight previews help you learn a line, and saved throw trails let you look back at a shot that worked. A career mode, offline daily challenges, practice, medals and personal records give you reasons to play the round again. Tokens earned in play unlock collection items. Settings cover assists, sound, music, vibration, reduced motion and a left-handed action layout. Core play works offline and your progress stays on your device. There are optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads between finished sessions. Plays in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
