Marble Mandate

Android Volume Store prep

The marbles keep coming. Flip the track switches and send each one to the matching bin.

Marble Mandate is a sorting game played on track switches. Marked marbles roll down the track one after another, and you tap switch buttons to send each one into the bin with the matching symbol. The catch is timing. A switch locks while a marble is crossing it, so you tap to queue the next direction and it flips the moment the fork is clear. Heavy marbles roll slower than the rest, merges need space, and the next arrivals are already rolling into view while you are still sorting the last one, so you plan one switch ahead. A practice mode with slower arrivals lets you learn the layouts at an easier pace. Bolts earned in play unlock marble color styles. Plays offline with no account, in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Free, with occasional ads between runs and optional rewarded ads for extra bolts.
