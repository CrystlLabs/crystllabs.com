Window Seat

PC Volume Live

Ride scenic trains, ferries and buses, and photograph what flashes past your window.

Mara finds a shoebox of postcards from her grandmother's year on the road in 1974, and each one says the same thing: find the view I saw. Ride trains, ferries and buses through six regions while the world rolls past. Drag to aim, zoom and shoot, but film is limited, so wait for the moment: the stag lifting its head, the lighthouse winking at dusk.
