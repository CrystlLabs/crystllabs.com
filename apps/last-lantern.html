Last Lantern

Android Volume Store prep

Tap left or right to parry. Wait out the breath, ignore the feints, keep the light on.

One light remains, and your job is to keep it burning. Last Lantern is a compact game of timing and attention. Attacks come from the left and the right, each with a warning, and you tap the matching side to parry. Time it to the ring for a perfect parry. Precise parries build a chain and the focus for a sweeping counter. Hollow symbols are feints that test your patience, and when the breath signal appears you stop tapping and let the lantern recover. Your lives are flames. There are 12 challenges, each cleared by completing 12 objectives: bronze for a clear, silver for losing at most one life, gold for losing none. Practice lets you learn the sequences at 70, 85 or 100 percent speed and keeps its results separate. Embers you earn unlock lantern color styles, which change only the look. Plays offline without an account, in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Free with occasional ads between runs and optional rewarded ads.
