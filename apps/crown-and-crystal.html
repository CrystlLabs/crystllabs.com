Crown & Crystal

PC Volume Live

Match gems, set off cascades, and restore a little puzzle kingdom.

Crown & Crystal is a match-3 puzzle game with bright jewels and a kingdom to restore. Swap gems on a 7 x 7 board, plan around collection goals and frosted tiles, and turn one clever move into a cascade with row and column beams, area bursts and special-gem swaps. Later campaign levels add frost patterns and thick ice that needs two hits. Stars from every finished puzzle bring back six kingdom districts, each with four restoration stages, in the order you choose. There is also a seeded Daily Sparkle puzzle and Moonlight mode, with no move limit and no targets.
