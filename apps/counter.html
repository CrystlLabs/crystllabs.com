Counter

Android Volume Live

Haggle with heroes who need coin tonight. Three offers, then they walk out.

Heroes come out of the hole with things they do not understand and rent due on Tuesday. You have a set of scales and no scruples. Counter is a dungeon shop game about the part nobody else simulates: naming the number. Buy low from people who cannot wait, sell high to people who cannot count. Shutters up to shutters down is two or three minutes. They say a number first and it is never their best one. Counter it, hold, or walk. Three exchanges and they go. Nothing ever tells you what a thing is worth, not the shelf, not the ledger, not the sale afterwards. Stock goes stale, and the rent is due whether the day went well or not.
