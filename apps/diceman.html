DICEMAN

Android Volume In development

Three dice, one decision. Outplay nine rivals and build a street-dice career.

DICEMAN is a tactical dice game with an offline career. You start with $50 in fictional cash and a leather jacket, and work your way from the corner to the top of three districts. A match is first to two rounds. Your rival locks a hand first. Then you roll three dice, tap to hold the ones you want, and spend limited Focus to reroll the rest. Triples beat straights, straights beat pairs, and pairs beat high totals. Your first roll each round is free, and Focus has to last the whole match. Nine rivals play in different styles. Train Focus, odds reads and composure between matches, earn three mastery marks per opponent, and beat district champions for their trophy chains. Collect 23 jackets, chains, watches, rings and dice sets; they are cosmetic and never change your rolls. Daily contracts, three-match Night Runs and free corner games fill out the career. No account and no purchases. All money is fictional, with no wagering or cash-out.
