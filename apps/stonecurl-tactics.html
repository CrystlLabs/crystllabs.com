Stonecurl Tactics

Android Volume Store prep

Pick the line, set the weight, sweep it to the button. Curling, played as a puzzle.

Stonecurl Tactics is curling built around decisions. For each stone you choose a line, set the delivery weight and sweep the shot toward the button. Collisions can move a guard or clear a scoring stone, so a single stone can change the whole end. Play quick two-end games, 60 tactical challenges, or a full eight-end arcade match with eight stones per team. Ice settings and four rival shot styles change what the right call is, and last-stone advantage switches after a scoring end. Tournament guard-protection rules are not enforced, so the game is about the shots, not the rulebook. Career progression, offline daily challenges, practice, medals and personal records give you reasons to come back, and tokens earned in play unlock collection items. Core play works offline and progress stays on your device. Available in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Free, with optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between sessions.
