Critter Drop

Android Volume Store prep

Drop round critters into a jar, merge matching pairs, and grow a chick into a whale.

Critter Drop is a calm drop-and-merge puzzle. Round critters fall into a jar, and when two of the same kind touch, they squish into the next size up. There are twelve of them, from a tiny chick all the way to a whale. Drag to aim and let go to drop. A guide shows where each critter will land, and the next one waits by the rim. The pile rolls and settles, and one good drop can set off a chain of merges. If a critter stays above the rim, the run ends, so you have to keep space free. The daily challenge gives everyone the same drops, with the goal of making a panda in 60. Every run pays coins toward 8 jars, including teacups, a fishbowl and a hot tub, and 8 backgrounds. No energy and no waiting. Plays offline with no account. Free, with ads between runs, never during play.
