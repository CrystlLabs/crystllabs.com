Dune Sail

Android Volume Store prep

No engine, just wind. Trim the sail and tack a land yacht through desert checkpoints.

Dune Sail is a racing game about three-wheeled land yachts. There is no engine, only wind, wheels and the timing of your turns. You steer through checkpoints, trim your sail and tack across the breeze to keep moving. Race 18 routes across three desert settings with nine yacht profiles, and take on 30 wind challenges. Assisted trim teaches you the wind window, while manual trim puts a sail slider in your hands. Save a local ghost of your best route and race against it. Endurance stretches a route into three laps and 18 checkpoints. Career progression, offline daily challenges, practice, medals and personal records track how you are doing, and tokens earned in play unlock collection items. Settings include assists, sound, music, vibration, reduced motion and a left-handed layout. Core play works offline and progress stays on your device. Free, with optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between sessions.
