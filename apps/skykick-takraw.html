Skykick Takraw

Android Volume Store prep

Receive, set, spike. Rooftop sepak takraw in seven-point matches.

Skykick Takraw is sepak takraw on the rooftops, the net game played with the feet. You find the rhythm of receive, set and spike: time three touches, aim for the open side of the court, and turn a rally into a point. Aim, feints and stamina make every seven-point match a fresh decision. An arcade circuit of 16 matches runs through five court settings, and you choose among 12 athlete profiles. Thirty combination challenges let you drill the touches on their own. A career mode, offline daily challenges, practice, medals and personal records keep you coming back, and tokens earned in play unlock collection items. Settings include assists, sound, music, vibration, reduced motion and a left-handed action layout. Core play works offline and progress stays on your device. Optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads between finished sessions. Plays in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
