Bento Balance

Android Volume Store prep

Stack rice, sushi and broccoli on a tilting tray. Serve the recipe before lunch tips.

Bento Balance turns lunch into a balancing act. Each round shows a recipe card, and you drop the bites onto a tray that tilts with every piece. Drag to pick where the food will fall, then let go. Rice, sushi and broccoli all sit differently, and a neat stack can turn into a very wobbly lunch fast. Serve a finished recipe and the food left on the tray changes the balance for the next one, so you are always planning a step ahead. Work through 18 recipe cards, or use practice to try stacks without losing napkins. Sesame tokens you earn unlock new tray colors. Plays offline with no account, in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Free, with occasional ads between runs and optional rewarded ads for extra tokens.
