Reef Relay

Android Volume Store prep

Draw one current and a whole school of fish follows it home. 24 reefs, four-second lines.

Reef Relay is a puzzle about steering a whole school of fish with one line. Draw a current, lift your finger to release it, and the fish follow it toward the arch that matches their badge. A current lasts four seconds, and drawing again replaces it. Round and slim fish turn at different speeds, so you leave room for the whole group. There are 24 reef boards with high and low exits, coral slaloms, overlapping schools, narrow currents and moving jellyfish. Badges use circle, triangle and square shapes, so color is never the only guide. Rescue twelve fish to clear a board for bronze, silver or gold, and save a whole school for bonus points and five extra seconds. Clear six boards to unlock Endless Current. Twelve school challenges pay pearls toward eight reef color themes, and a playable tutorial gets you started. Plays offline with no account. Free with ads, and ads never rescue fish, change medals or unlock boards.
