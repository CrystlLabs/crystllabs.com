Terrace Kings

PC Volume Live

A pixel football career of promotion battles, loyalty and dangerous deals.

Your neighborhood club is being hollowed out by a transfer broker. Rise from academy player to captain and decide who the badge belongs to. Play two full halves of 11-a-side football with passes, through balls, crosses, shooting, defensive choices and substitutions. Cup fixtures can go to extra time and penalties, and you choose your club's preparation and formation before kickoff. Away from the match, work through 36 story encounters with 12 recurring characters, where choices shape trust, debt, community support and relationships, and earlier decisions come back later. Balance training, recovery, sponsors and club improvements across a 12-fixture season, with three career backgrounds and retirement legacies.
