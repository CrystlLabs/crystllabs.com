Seed & Stone

PC Volume Live

Small seeds. A garden reborn.

Join Asha as she returns to a community garden whose water channels have run dry, meet its keepers and restore five gardens one game of Kalah at a time. Pick up the stones from one of your six cups and sow them counterclockwise into your store, end in your store to play again, and capture from the cup opposite when your last stone lands in an empty cup on your side. A 40-match story across five gardens comes with Asha's illustrated seed journal, along with 60 sowing challenges and a daily sowing puzzle, against relaxed, balanced or expert keepers or a second player.
