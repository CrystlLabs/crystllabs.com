Pixel Precinct

PC Volume Live

Find hidden clues in pixel crime scenes, catch the liar and close 30 cases.

Rookie detective Sam Reyes has just joined the 13th Precinct of Maple Heights, a cosy pixel city where someone leaves a tiny pixel calling card with a single letter at every crime scene. Search busy pixel-art rooms for the objects on your list, solve lab puzzles with fingerprints, torn photos and coded notes, then question three suspects and pick the liar and the evidence that proves it. There are 30 written cases across six districts, 90 hand-built crime scenes, thirty calling cards that spell out a secret, five ways to replay any scene, a patrol mode and a daily scene.
