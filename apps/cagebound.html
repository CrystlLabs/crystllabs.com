Cagebound

PC Volume Live

An MMA deckbuilder where every card is a technique. Read the fight, save the gym.

Ironwood MMA is running out of time, so Coach Dell Marsh enters three fighters in the Apex Grand Prix to save the gym. Every card is a real technique, tied to standing range, the clinch or the ground. You see your opponent's next move before you commit, so the fight is about reading and position: target head, body or legs, take top control and chain setups into a finish. 160 cards, three styles, eight fights.
