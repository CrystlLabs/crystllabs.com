Kickflip Courier

Android Volume Store prep

Grab the parcels, grind the rails, land the trick. Arrive with at least one box.

Kickflip Courier is a skateboard delivery game. You steer through a colorful skatepark, collect golden parcels, jump obstacles and link aerial tricks on the way to the finish. Ollie, then swipe in one of eight directions for a trick, and hold grind as you land on a rail to build your score. Keep your combo alive, follow the distance meter, and arrive with at least one parcel. Six route patterns develop into 36 short delivery routes, and there is a daily delivery challenge on top. Practice lets you try any route before it is unlocked, and campaign medals and personal records stay separate from practice. Board color finishes unlock as you go. Play offline with no account, with progress and personal bests kept on your device. Interstitial ads show at breaks between sessions, and optional rewarded videos only give cosmetic rewards. Plays in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
