Orbit Latch

Android Volume Store prep

Hold to latch onto an anchor, swing, release. One touch, 36 sectors, 12 lessons.

Orbit Latch is a one-touch space game about timing and momentum. Hold to catch the highlighted gravity anchor, swing around it, then release toward the next ring. A dotted tangent guide shows your flight path. Stay attached too long and the tether overheats. There are 36 authored sectors in twelve families: wide orbits, compact slingshots, high and low anchors, half-circle escapes, asteroid corridors, moving anchors, one-way gates and three-anchor finales. Endless Flight mixes them into varied routes. Twelve four-gate lessons award bronze, silver and gold medals. Center passes earn extra points, and gold needs four centers without a heat break. Star dust buys eight hull-color and trail styles that never change your speed or abilities. A playable tutorial and practice that costs nothing help you learn. Sound, vibration, reduced motion and menu text size are adjustable. Offline, no account, in English, Spanish or Brazilian Portuguese. Free with occasional ads between runs, and an optional ad can double a run's star dust once.
