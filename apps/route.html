Route

Android Volume Live

Six machines, one van, one day. Restock the round and set a price.

Six vending machines. One van. One day. You drive the round, see what sold since you were last there, restock out of the back, and set one price at every stop. Load the van once, because it will not hold enough for everything. Nothing you decide today is settled today: the price you set at a machine is what it pays out the next time you stand in front of it. Six stops is about three minutes, and the last one is midnight. Six streets that want different things, a roof lot that is nearly dead until it is hot out, weather that moves all of it, and every pitch charging rent whether you stocked it or not. It is not idle. Nothing earns while you are away.
