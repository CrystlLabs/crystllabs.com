Royal Race

PC Volume Live

Seven runners. One invitation for everyone.

Carry Nara's invitation through five river districts in the Royal Game of Ur, one of the oldest board games known. Throw four tetrahedral dice, race your seven runners along a shared road, land on rosettes for another throw and send your rival back to the start. Bring all seven runners home with an exact roll to win. A 35-race story comes with Nara's illustrated city journal, along with 50 risk studies that show the odds of the next roll and a daily challenge, against the computer on three levels or a second player.
