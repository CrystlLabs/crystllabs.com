Murder Phone 2

Android Volume Store prep

Someone is posting you dead people's phones. Read them, text the contacts, find the liar.

Somebody is posting you phones. Each one belonged to a person who is not coming back for it. The lock screen still shows the minute it stopped, and the messages are still unread. Murder Phone 2 is a text detective game. You read a stranger's phone, text the people in it, and catch the one whose story does not hold. Every reply is a written choice, so careful reading is the whole skill. Show someone the photo that contradicts them. Forward one person's message to another and watch the story change. Put the night back in order on a timeline board, and pin two statements against each other on a corkboard. Contacts have moods and remember how you spoke to them. When you know, name one person and the evidence that proves it. Eleven phones: one to learn on, eight that form a season, two that stand alone. Offline, no account. Free with ads. The cases are fiction.
