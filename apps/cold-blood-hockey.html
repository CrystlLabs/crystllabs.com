Cold Blood Hockey

PC Volume Live

Play full hockey games, lead your line and fight for your town's rink.

Your frozen mill town has one rink and one shot at the cup. Become its captain while an ownership syndicate engineers a move to a richer city. Play three full periods with passing, shots, checking, substitutions, icing and offside, where penalties change manpower and tied games can reach overtime. Prepare a scoring line or a checking unit while protecting the town's rink. Away from the match, work through 36 story encounters with 12 recurring characters, where choices shape trust, debt, community support and relationships, and earlier decisions come back later. Balance training, recovery, sponsors and club improvements across a 12-fixture season, with three career backgrounds and retirement legacies.
