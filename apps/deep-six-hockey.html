Deep Six Hockey

Android Volume Store prep

Underwater hockey. Pass, dive, score, and come up for air before your swimmer runs out.

Deep Six Hockey is arcade underwater hockey. You control the puck, swim into passing lanes, find a teammate and flick toward goal, all while keeping an eye on your air. Surface to recover breath, but a swimmer at the surface cannot carry the puck, so the real skill is switching to a rested teammate at the right moment. Pick wide, pressing or defensive formations to change how your team moves. Matches end at five goals or after 150 seconds. There are 36 career stages and 36 challenges across six pool settings, plus offline daily challenges, practice, medals and personal records. Tokens earned in play unlock collection items. Settings cover assists, sound, music, vibration, reduced motion and a left-handed layout. Core play works offline and progress stays on your device. Free, with optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between finished sessions.
