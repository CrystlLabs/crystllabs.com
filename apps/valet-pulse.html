Valet Pulse

Android Volume Store prep

Four lanes, growing queues, one crossing. Release each car at the right moment.

Valet Pulse is a timing game about a busy crossing. Cars queue up in four lanes, and nothing moves until you tap a lane to release one car. Hold to preview its route first, then choose the moment it enters the intersection and leave it enough room to pass safely. Vans need extra space, so wait until one has cleared. Temporary closures change what is open, and every queue has to stay below six cars. Each safe arrival helps your shift, while an impatient launch costs one of your service hearts. A shift is cleared with at least 10 parked cars and one heart left. Medals go bronze for a clear, silver for losing at most one heart and gold for losing none. There are 12 challenges, a relaxed 120-second shift, practice, and an Endless mode that opens after six clears. Tickets you earn unlock vehicle color styles. Plays offline without an account, in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Free with occasional ads between runs and optional rewarded ads.
