Fronton Fever

Android Volume Store prep

Catch it in the cesta, sling it at the front wall, and read the rebound. Arcade jai alai.

Fronton Fever is fast arcade jai alai. You catch the ball in your cesta, aim at the front wall and sling the next shot past your rival. Pick a drive, a lob, a side-wall shot or a backhand, then move to meet the return before its second bounce on the floor. Matches are seven-point duels, and optional catching assistance helps while you learn the rhythm of the walls. Play 40 career stages and 40 rebound challenges across six fronton settings, plus offline daily challenges, practice, medals and personal records. Tokens earned through play unlock collection items. Settings include assists, sound, music, vibration, reduced motion and a left-handed action layout. Core play works offline and progress stays on your device. Available in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Free, with optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between finished sessions.
