Bare Knuckle

Android Volume Live

No gloves, no rounds, no referee. Every punch costs the hand that threw it.

You fight for money in car parks and pub back rooms. There are no gloves, no rounds and no referee, so nothing ends a fight except one man staying down. Every punch you throw damages the hand that threw it, which means the punch that wins the fight is usually the punch that breaks your hand. A broken hand is career damage. One fight is four to six minutes. Between bouts you pick the next fight off the circuit board and spend the purse on your hands, your corner or your debts. The crowd is the meter and it pays: a short violent win is worth more than an ugly grinding one. The career ends in a body, not a belt.
