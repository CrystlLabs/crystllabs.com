Boo's Little Memories

PC Volume Live

Some things are worth remembering.

Help Boo, a gentle ghost, remember its favorite things. Solve nonograms to reveal teacups, teddy bears and gloriously wonky cakes, then use them to furnish a cozy attic. The numbers show consecutive groups of filled squares, with at least one empty square between groups, so fill squares, cross out the ones that stay empty and solve every row and column to bring back each memory. There is no timer. 200 picture-logic puzzles on 5×5, 10×10 and 15×15 grids and 48 keepsake pictures to arrange in Boo's attic.
