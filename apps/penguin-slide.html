Penguin Slide

Android Volume Store prep

Tap to turn at every corner of a twisty ice path. Miss one and it is into the snow.

Penguin Slide is a one-tap endless game about a round little penguin sliding on his belly down a twisty ice path. He only goes where you tell him. Every tap sends him sliding the other way with a spray of snow. Turn at each corner and he keeps zooming along. Turn too early or too late and he slides off the edge and tumbles into the snow. Every ice tile you pass is a point, and fish sit on some tiles for you to scoop up. The longer you last, the faster he goes: straight runs get shorter, corners come quicker, and later thin ice appears, narrow tiles that only hold if you stay in the middle. Fish buy 10 cube pets to slide with, including a polar bear, a parrot and a koala. Free with ads, nothing to buy, no account, plays offline. Optional ads give a second chance, double fish or a free pet.
