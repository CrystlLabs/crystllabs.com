Samba Notes

PC Volume Live

Match music notes, write the samba and bring a broke samba school back to Carnaval.

A samba school lost everything: the drums, the costumes, the dancers. With 300 days to Carnaval, Bia and her grandmother set out to write a new samba one note at a time. Swap notes to match three or more, make power notes, blasts and the color-clearing pandeiro, and every win writes a bar of the song. 300 levels across 10 chapters, all the way to the Sambódromo.
