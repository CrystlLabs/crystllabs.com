Twistworld

PC Volume Live

A tiny kingdom lives on a cube. Twist it back together, or speed-solve.

Quadra is a tiny kingdom on the six faces of a cube, and a sulky storm spirit has twisted it out of shape. Roads end in rivers and rivers run up hills. Each face is a 3x3 map of tiles: drag a row to twist a layer, just like a classic cube, and the roads and rivers move between faces. Reconnect the paths so Pip the mail runner can finish the route.
