Night Receipt

Android Volume Live

A convenience store mystery where a receipt arrives stamped with tomorrow's date.

Your first night should be ordinary: open the till, scan groceries and count the change. Then a receipt arrives stamped tomorrow. Night Receipt is a first-person mystery in a small roadside convenience store. Compare what you remember with what is there now, find thirty-five distinct anomalies and check four live security cameras. Meet twelve recurring customers and follow Elise's disappearance across seven chapters, keeping receipts, preserving evidence for Officer Han or following the owner's unsettling rules. Your records and choices open four endings, including a route that is always available.
