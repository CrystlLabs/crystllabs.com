Mask Protocol

PC Volume Live

Plan three thieves, outsmart guards and escape in anime stealth tactics.

Mask Protocol is a turn-based stealth strategy game where Vera's crew plans together, moves together and escapes together. Move three thieves across a readable tactical board, preview patrol directions and vision tiles, assign gadgets and undo anything you have not executed yet. When you execute, the whole crew moves first, then the guards move and look. Six crew roles and gadgets like smoke, EMP, cloaks and decoys open new routes. Complete 18 campaign heists across three venues, grab optional gems and handle five guard behaviors, then try daily and endless operations.
