Northmark Ski Orienteering

Android Volume Store prep

Read the trail map, pick the junctions, and reach every numbered control in order.

Northmark is ski orienteering, where the quickest route starts with a good decision. You read a trail map and visit numbered controls in order, choosing which connected junction to take next and comparing how much effort each trail costs. Sprinting is faster but drains your energy, so you decide when to push and when to rest. Zoom the map to plan, then follow your own route trace. There are 40 ski navigation courses across five region settings, and practice lets you explore without a time limit. A career mode, offline daily challenges, practice, medals and personal records keep you coming back, and tokens earned in play unlock collection items. Settings include assists, sound, music, vibration, reduced motion and a left-handed action layout. Core play works offline and progress stays on your device. Optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads between finished sessions. Plays in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
