Mochi Stack

Android Volume Store prep

Tap to drop a squishy cube pet onto the tower. Land it dead center for a PERFECT.

Mochi Stack is a one-tap stacking game with cute animals. A little cloud swings a cube pet on a rope from side to side. Tap, and the pet drops onto the tower below with a soft boing. Land anywhere on the pet underneath and it stays for a point. Land dead center for a PERFECT: double points, a coin and a burst of sparkles, and chained perfects make the tower glow. Miss, or hang too far over the edge, and your pet tumbles off. The higher you go, the faster and wider the swing, the more the tower sways, and later a gust of wind can push your pet sideways. The sky changes as you climb, from sunny meadow to sunset to a starry night. Coins buy 12 cube pets, from a cat and a panda to a fox. Free with ads, nothing to buy, no account, plays offline.
