Rooftop Rim

Android Volume Store prep

Five-ball racks, bank shots and moving hoops on 40 courts above the city.

Rooftop Rim is a basketball shooting game set on rooftops above a warm-lit city. Every attempt gives you a rack of five balls. Drag down to set power, aim sideways and release. The power meter and the dots at the start of your arc show where the shot is heading. Score a clean swish or bank it off the backboard, and keep a streak going for bonus points. There are 40 short court challenges, and they ask you to adapt to new shooting positions, longer distances and hoops that move. Bronze, silver and gold medals mark your best runs, and a shot-by-shot scorecard shows every result. Try any court in practice, work through the campaign, or play the daily rack. Tokens from play unlock ball colors. Practice and assisted scores stay separate from campaign medals and personal records. No account, and progress is saved on your device. English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Free with ads at session breaks, plus an optional video for a three-ball practice rack.
