Jurassic Pixel

Android Volume Live

Pair two of a kind, check the odds, hatch the egg. 17 species, 85 colors to breed.

Your grandfather built a dinosaur park and never finished it. He left you a few pens, a hatchery and his breeding ledger: seventeen species, five colors each, most rows still blank. Pair two dinosaurs of the same kind and wait out the egg. The hatchling takes its color from its parents, usually one or the other and now and then a color neither had. The Hatchery shows the odds for every color before you pair, so the gamble is yours to plan. Hatchlings grow into adults on a real clock, app open or not. Sessions are short on purpose. Your dinosaurs bank coins while the app is closed, so you open it, collect, pay for the next pairing and close it again. Eggs take two to forty minutes. Five enclosures, six letters from your grandfather, thirty-five goals and a name for every dinosaur. Nothing dies, nothing gets sick, and a pairing never fails. Offline, no account, free with ads, no purchases. English, Korean, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
