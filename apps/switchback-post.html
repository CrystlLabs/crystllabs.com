Switchback Post

Android Volume Store prep

Ski the parcel downhill through the right gates and reach the cabin with the seal intact.

Switchback Post is a one-finger skiing delivery game. You carry one parcel down a mountain full of choices. Drag to steer, pass the gates whose symbols match your delivery route, and finish at the marked cabin with the parcel intact. Read the next gate early, dodge the trees, and use repair flags to recover a damaged seal before the finish. There are 36 delivery challenges, each played on three lives: clear one for bronze, lose at most one life for silver, lose none for gold. Clearing six boards opens Endless mode, and practice lets you rehearse a route with its results kept separate. Stamps earned in play unlock color styles. Play offline without an account, with progress and personal records saved on your device. Ads appear between runs, and optional rewarded ads give extra stamps. Plays in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
