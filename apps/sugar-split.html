Sugar Split

Android Volume Store prep

Two gumdrops, opposite gravities, one shared tap. Every flip moves both lanes.

Sugar Split is a one-tap arcade game about two gumdrops with opposite gravities. One tap flips both of them at once, so every flip changes both lanes. You watch both characters, read the paired openings and obstacles, and pick a moment that works for the pair, not just for one. Passing matching rings with both gumdrops earns a Harmony bonus, or you can ignore the rings and focus on getting both through the next obstacle. There are 48 challenges, each played on three lives: clear one for bronze, lose at most one life for silver, lose none for gold. Clearing six boards opens Endless mode. Practice runs slower and keeps its results separate. Sprinkles earned in play unlock gumdrop color styles. Play offline without an account, with progress and personal records saved on your device. Ads appear between runs, and optional rewarded ads give extra sprinkles. Plays in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
