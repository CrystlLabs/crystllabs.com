Solitaire Postcards

Android Volume Store prep

Draw-one Klondike across illustrated destinations. The daily deal always has a solution.

Solitaire Postcards is classic draw-one Klondike set against a journey of illustrated destinations. You build descending runs in alternating colors on the tableau, move cards up to same-suit foundations from ace to king, turn over hidden cards, and draw one card at a time from the stock. You can recycle the stock as often as you need. Play by tapping or dragging. Six illustrated destination settings carry the journey, and a daily deal always has a solution. Journey deals are shuffled fairly, which means some of them cannot be won. You can undo recent moves freely and use three free hints per round. Once every card is face up and ready, the rest collect themselves. Your board, stock and progress are saved when you leave. Core card play works offline, with no account and no purchases. Optional rewarded videos give extra hints, and occasional interstitial ads show between finished rounds.
