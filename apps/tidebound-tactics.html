Tidebound Tactics

PC Volume Live

Aim clever cannon ricochets and command an anime fleet in naval strategy.

Tidebound Tactics is a turn-based naval strategy game where captain Marin leads a small fleet through tight island battles. Move either of your two ships, aim and fire, banking shots off stone barriers while managing wind and currents. The shot preview uses the same simulation as the cannonball, and bank shots hit harder. Pick from eight hulls and six captains, draft from 20 modules after each win and switch between four ammunition types. Clear 24 campaign passages with five boss fleets, then try the daily expedition or endless battles.
