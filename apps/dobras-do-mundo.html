Dobras do Mundo

PC Volume Live

The map is your most powerful tool.

The path is also on the other side of the sheet. Help Lia and the paper fox Nilo recover her grandmother's atlas by folding terrain, joining paths and carrying memories to the lighthouse. Drag a crease to pick the flap that turns over, preview the result and confirm the fold. Folding reflects positions, flips faces and reverses stacks, and up to three folds can be stacked. There are 48 story pages across four communities that introduce paper, foil, cloth and vellum, each with its own folding rules, plus five-page expeditions, a daily atlas and a map workshop where you build, test and share your own pages.
