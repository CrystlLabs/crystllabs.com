Shabd Steps

PC Volume Live

A little word. A new journal page.

A picture, a clue and a word waiting to be found. Shabd Steps fills a traveller's journal with short word puzzles about everyday objects and places, and Shabd means "word" in Hindi. Read the clue or look at the picture, then click the letter tiles to build the word. In a word ladder, change one tile at each rung to make the next word. Each language has its own 300 puzzles: 210 anagrams, 60 picture puzzles with original illustrations and 30 word ladders. Every word you solve goes into your journal to review later, and a daily ladder waits every day.
