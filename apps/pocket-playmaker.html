Pocket Playmaker

Android Volume Store prep

Draw a three-pass move through the defense, then curl the finish around the keeper.

Pocket Playmaker turns each soccer possession into a small puzzle. You read the moving defense, find a passing lane and bend your finish around the keeper. Start on the player with the bright ring, drag through up to three teammates and release to queue the passes, or make each pass separately. Then drag from the receiver into the goal to shoot, aiming left or right to curve the ball. The tactical clock slows while you draw a route, and defender footprints, a shot guide and a ball trail help you read the play. Each round is three possessions of 30 seconds, and you need at least one goal to clear it. There are 60 arrangements across four stadium looks and six defender movement styles, plus practice, bronze to gold medals, a daily challenge and an endless mode. Tokens unlock team color tints that do not change player power. Plays offline with no account. Free, with ads at breaks between sessions.
