PC Volume Live

Book wrestling shows, build rivalries and grow a pixel promotion. Play offline.

Gil Moreno left you four wrestlers, a ring, a television deal with twelve weeks remaining and a pile of debt. Build a promotion from a church hall to a stadium, one Friday night at a time, while Marlene keeps the books and rival promoter Chase Montgomery eyes your talent. Book one to four matches per show, choose winners and finishes, call spots during animated pixel matches and build feuds toward a supercard payoff. Manage 64 fictional wrestlers, 12 match types, five venues and four championships across a ten-year career.
