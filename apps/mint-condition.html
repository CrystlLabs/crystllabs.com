Mint Condition

PC Volume Live

Grade, trade and display fantasy cards in a cozy collector's shop.

An empty page in an old binder belonged to the 1998 Lantern Cup. Run a small card shop, meet the regulars and find out what happened to the missing card. Each shop event is a short puzzle: inspect a card's corners, edges, surface and centering and grade it, trade with collectors who have favourite sets and fixed limits, then pick three cards for the night's exhibition. 120 original fantasy cards in six sets, 20 shop events, 12 collectors, 24 journal memories, six story chapters and three endings.
