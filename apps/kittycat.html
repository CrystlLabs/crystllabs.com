Kittycat

Android Volume Store prep

Bring home a shy cat and earn their trust, one small day at a time.

Kittycat is a cozy cat game about care, stories and turning a quiet room into a home. Adopt curious Miso, cautious Juniper or mischievous Mochi, and give them any name. Each day you pick from six small moments: serve supper, play, brush a fluffy coat, sit together, take a nap or explore. You learn which foods and toys your cat loves, then share the next story chapter and choose how to help. There are 14 chapters, with a thunderstorm, a stolen sock, an afternoon that goes too quiet and new friends. Your choices are remembered and lead to one of three endings, and the days go on after the last chapter. Two touch minigames let you chase a toy or follow a pattern. Buttons you earn buy a cushion, a scratching post, a window perch and more, and every shared moment goes into the memory book. Three save slots. No neglect penalties: your cat rests safely while you are away. No account, no chat, no purchases. Free with ads.
