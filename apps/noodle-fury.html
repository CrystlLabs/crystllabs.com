Noodle Fury

Android Volume Live

Stick on the left, fists on the right. Get Grandma's broth back before it goes cold.

Noodle Fury is an arcade beat 'em up built for two thumbs. The Noodle Ninjas broke into Grandma's ramen shop and took her secret broth recipe, her cat Mochi and a pork bun. Kenji sets off with a hot delivery in one hand, and the soup has to arrive before it goes cold. Put your left thumb anywhere on the left and a stick appears under it. On the right, tap HIT and keep tapping for a three-hit combo, hold BLOCK when a goon winds up, and fire SUPER when the bar fills to clear the screen. Kenji only fights when you tell him to. There are 15 stages across five chapters, from back alleys and a neon arcade to the rooftops and Instant Empire Tower, with five bosses including Big Udon and Mr. Cup. Coins buy Health, Power, Super and Luck at Grandma's dojo. Stages take a minute or two. Offline, no account, optional ads, no purchases.
