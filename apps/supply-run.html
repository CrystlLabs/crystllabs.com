Supply Run

Android Volume Live

One bag, a meter that only goes up, and a door you can take at any time.

One house. One bag. A noise meter that only ever goes up. Supply Run is a scavenging game about knowing when to leave. Three houses on offer, each with a size, a risk and a rumour from whoever was in last. Pick one, go in, and carry out what you can before the house hears you. Two or three minutes, door to door. Tap anything with a drawer, a door or a lid, and every one you open is heard. The bag holds weight, not things, so a heavy item is two light ones you are not carrying. Get out with the bag and it is yours. Fill the meter first and you lose the bag. The nearer the meter is to full, the quieter it gets.
