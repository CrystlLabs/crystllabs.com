Sticky Fingers

Android Volume Store prep

A pickpocket game in a busy market. Lift wallets while nobody is looking.

Sticky Fingers is a pickpocket game set in a busy market square. A crowd drifts between the stalls, every person has a wallet, and every person has eyes. Drag to walk through the plaza. Stand next to someone and hold on them for half a second to lift their wallet. If any yellow sight cone falls on you while you hold, you are spotted and the lift fails. Nobody gets hurt: three spots and the round is over. Each round lasts one minute. Every day has a mark, a grey-haired gent with a gold ring around his feet and $90 in his wallet. The crowd grows every round, up to 22 people. From round 6 a bodyguard shadows the mark, and from round 12 a rival thief works the same crowd. Earn three stars per round by surviving, lifting the mark and never getting spotted. The old man's attic fills with 40 pieces of street-market furniture. One thumb. Free with optional rewarded ads. No purchases, no account.
