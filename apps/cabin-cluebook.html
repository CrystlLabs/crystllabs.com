Cabin Cluebook

PC Volume Live

A lakeside cabin and the keepsake Grandma hid for you.

Every summer of Lina's childhood was spent at Birchwater, Grandma Elin's cabin by the lake. Elin is gone now, and her will says only: "The music box is where we sang." Every room is a real watercolour by Carl Larsson from his 1890s series "A Home", and Elin hid clues in her journals, photographs and keepsakes. Solve twenty-five gentle puzzles: match old photographs to where they were taken, use and combine keepsakes, and decode letters in the sisters' secret code. A family story of a wartime engagement and a sister who sailed to America and did not write for sixteen years leads you to the music box.
