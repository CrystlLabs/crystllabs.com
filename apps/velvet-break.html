Velvet Break

PC Volume Live

Rise through the tennis tour, master each surface and protect your story.

A qualifier with a borrowed racket enters an exclusive tour where access is traded like currency. Build your game without letting the circuit own your story. Play complete best-of-three matches with serves, directional strokes, slices, volleys and lobs, handle deuce, tiebreaks and fatigue, and choose a surface and a serving or control-focused game. Away from the match, work through 36 story encounters with 12 recurring characters, where choices shape trust, debt, community support and relationships, and earlier decisions come back later. Balance training, recovery, sponsors and club improvements across a 12-fixture season, with three career backgrounds and retirement legacies.
