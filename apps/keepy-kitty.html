Keepy Kitty

Android Volume Store prep

Tap so the kitten hops and boops the balloon with her nose. Keep it off the grass.

Keepy Kitty is a one-tap balloon game with a kitten. A pink balloon drifts down over a sunny meadow, and kitty trots after it on her own. Tap and she hops up and boops it with her nose. Every boop is a point, and if the balloon touches the grass, it pops. Where she hits it matters: boop the left side and it floats off to the right, hit it dead center and it goes highest. Tap too early and she hops at nothing and needs a moment before trying again, so timing beats tapping fast. The longer you last, the faster the balloon falls. Wind gusts push it sideways, bees buzz across the sky, and later a second balloon joins in. Steer the balloon into floating strawberries for coins and spend them on new pets, from a chick and a bunny to a panda and a penguin. Free with optional ads, nothing to buy, no account, and it plays offline once installed.
