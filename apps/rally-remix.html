Rally Remix

Android Volume Store prep

Portrait tennis with automatic footwork. You handle the spin, drop shots and lobs.

Rally Remix is a portrait tennis game where footwork is automatic and shot placement is up to you. Swipe up to serve and return when the timing ring turns green. Swipe sideways for direction, vary the length for depth, or draw a curve to add spin, and choose Drive, Drop shot or Lob before your next swipe. Twelve named rivals play four styles: baseline builders, net rushers, angle hunters and deep defenders. Grass keeps the bounce low, clay sends it higher and hard courts play at their own pace. Rivals have to reach the ball to return it, so placement and shot choice matter. Matches are quick tie-breaks to seven with a two-point lead, and at 11-11 the next point decides it. Earn medals to unlock later rivals, practice against anyone, or take on a daily challenge. Tokens buy racket colors with no gameplay advantage. Plays offline with no account, no purchases and no online multiplayer. Free, with ads between matches.
