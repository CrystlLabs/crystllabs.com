Reading Room

Android Volume Live

Somebody sits down, says one thing, hides another. You decide what it means.

Nobody here can see the future, including you. Somebody sits down. They tell you one thing about themselves and hide a second one. Three cards go down. You decide, out loud, what they mean, and they pay whatever they thought it was worth. Reading Room is a tarot shop game about listening. Four clients a day, about thirty-five seconds each. No grid, no clock inside a reading, nothing to tap quickly. The only pressure in the room is the person across the table. What they want is in the first thing they say. Every card offers more than one reading, so turn it and pick the tone: kind, blunt, or the interesting one. You never see a face, only a silhouette and a pair of hands.
