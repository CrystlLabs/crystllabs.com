Corner Store

Android Volume Live

Order the stock. Open the doors. Watch the shift. A pixel art shop sim.

You are the shop manager of a corner store the size of one room. Every morning you fill in the order sheet: milk, bread, crisps, cola, whatever you think today wants. Then the doors open and you find out. This is not a game about tapping customers. Once the shift starts you cannot change your mind. If a shelf is empty they leave. If the queue gets long they leave anyway. You get three trips to the back room and no more. At close the receipt prints out of the register one line at a time: every sale, every walkout, every crate of milk that went off. Demand moves week to week, so last week's order sheet is a bad guess. A shift takes about three minutes.
