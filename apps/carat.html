Carat

Android Volume Store prep

Drag gem pieces onto an 8x8 velvet board. Fill a line and it shatters.

Carat is a block puzzle with jewels. Each turn you get three gem pieces to drag onto an 8x8 velvet board. Fill a row or a column and it shatters into sparkle. Before you let go, the lines a piece would complete light up, so you can see the clear coming. Clear two lines at once, or clear on several moves in a row, and the score climbs fast. When none of your three pieces fits, the run is over. The deals are fair: if any shape fits the board, one of your pieces does. Besides endless runs there are 120 levels where you free relic shards set in rough stone within a move limit, with up to three stars each, and a daily challenge with a new target score. Coins buy gem styles and velvet frames, nine in all. Plays offline, no account needed. Free with ads between rounds, never during play. No purchases.
