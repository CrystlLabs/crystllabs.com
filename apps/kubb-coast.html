Kubb Coast

Android Volume Store prep

Underarm throws, wooden kubbs, and a king you must not touch until the very end.

Kubb Coast is the wooden lawn game kubb, played across eight island settings. You throw batons underarm to clear the field kubbs, then attack the baseline, and leave the king for last, because hitting him early loses the game on the spot. Fallen kubbs get thrown back into the far half, so every six-baton turn sets up the next one. There are 50 career stages and 50 challenges, five rival accuracy profiles to play against, and local two-player games on one shared screen. Arcade matches are capped at twelve turns. A career mode, offline daily challenges, practice, medals and personal records keep you coming back, and tokens earned in play unlock collection items. Settings include assists, sound, music, vibration, reduced motion and a left-handed action layout. Core play works offline and progress stays on your device. Optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads between finished sessions. Plays in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
