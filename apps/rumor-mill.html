Rumor Mill

Android, PC Volume Live

Whisper one kind rumor to the right villager and steer it to the right ears.

You are Wren, the newest apprentice of the Whispering Society, a secret circle that has kept the village of Millbrook at peace for two hundred years. Whisper one kind rumor to the right villager at the right hour, then play the day hour by hour as people meet and share news. It must reach the Target before sunset while the Wrong Ears never hear a word. 120 story days across five seasons, 20 Scandal Days, 24 villagers and 72 secrets. Mend a feud between baker siblings and unmask a crooked tax collector, without anyone learning who started a rumor.
