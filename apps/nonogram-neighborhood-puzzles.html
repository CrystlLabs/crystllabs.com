Nonogram Neighborhood

Android Volume Live

Follow the number clues and fill the grid until a little pixel neighborhood appears.

Nonogram Neighborhood is a picture logic puzzle. The numbers beside each row and column give the lengths of its filled groups, and you work out which squares to fill and which to cross out until a small pixel-art scene appears: a rooftop, a window, a garden. Every puzzle is checked to have a complete logical solution, so you never have to guess. You start on 5x5 pictures and move up to 8x8 and 10x10 grids across a journey of rooftop, home and garden silhouettes, with a separate daily picture on top. Switch between fill and cross brushes or drag to mark a whole line, zoom the board for bigger squares, and use free reveals and undo when you get stuck. Your picture, brush and hints are saved when you leave. Core puzzles work offline, with no account and no in-app purchases. Optional rewarded videos give extra reveals, and occasional ads show between finished rounds.
