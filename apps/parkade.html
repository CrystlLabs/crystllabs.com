Parkade

Android Volume Store prep

Every car is boxed in. Tap them out in the right order, and do not bump anyone.

Parkade is a parking jam puzzle seen from straight above. Every car in the lot is boxed in, and each has an arrow on its roof. Tap a car and it drives the way its arrow points, either out onto the street or into whatever is in its way. Find the car that can leave first, then the next, until the lot is empty. A car that meets another bumps, honks and rolls back to its spot. That counts as a crash, and three crashes end the lot, so clear it with none for three stars. There are 120 lots, from 8 cars up to 40, and every one is checked to be clearable without a crash. Two-way cars show up from lot 21, where you swipe to pick a direction, and exit gates from lot 41. There is also a daily lot, day, dusk, snow and night lots, and paint sets for the cars. Plays offline with no account. Free, with an ad between some lots and no purchases.
