Ember Kitchen

PC Volume Live

Cook anime comfort food, build your kitchen and chain a perfect service.

Ember Kitchen is an anime cooking and time-management game starring Akari, a chef who turns a smart counter layout into a smooth dinner rush. Prepare ingredients, manage heat and serve before patience runs out. Rearrange nine counters between services, since station adjacency can speed preparation, improve quality or cool nearby work. Master 30 named recipes with three- or four-step station chains, balance three simultaneous orders and chain clean dishes into combos and Ember Fever. Play 20 campaign services across five chapters, then return for the daily challenge or an endless shift.
