Tiny Tonics

Android Volume Store prep

Pour the layers until every flask is one color, cork it, and hand it over the counter.

Tiny Tonics is a color sort puzzle in a pixel potion shop. Tap a flask to pick it up and tap another to pour. The top color only pours into an empty flask or onto the same color, as many layers as fit. A flask full of one color gets its cork, and when every flask is corked or empty, a customer walks in and takes the potions. There are 120 levels, from 3 colors up to 12, each one checked solvable by a solver, growing from five flasks to fourteen in two rows. From level 31, mystery layers stay hidden until they reach the top. You get three free undos per level and a restart any time, and there is no timer. A daily puzzle arrives every day. Fewer pours earn more stars, and coins from each level buy 24 decorations for the shop, from plants to pets to lanterns. Plays offline, no account. Ads show between levels, never during play.
