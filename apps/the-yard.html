The Yard

Android Volume Live

Survive the sentence, fight smart, earn parole, or risk a prison-yard escape.

Thirty days on the board. Three choices each day. One way out, if you can find it. The Yard is a gritty prison survival RPG built around hard daily decisions. Work details earn cigarettes and parole favor but drain your energy. Keeping your head down restores health and lowers suspicion. Building support opens a dangerous shortcut, while a clean record can bring freedom closer at a hearing. When a yard fight starts, brute force is not enough: read your rival's stance, guard the wide right, jab while they circle, and attack the body when their hands rise. Spend carefully at the commissary. Suspicion changes the odds, and failure adds days to the sentence.
