Tideline Salvage

Android Volume Store prep

Read the tides, send the fleet out, restore what comes back. A harbor salvage sim.

Tideline Salvage is a salvage and restoration sim set on a sleepy island harbor. You build dock space, a restoration workshop and storage, then open Operations to pick a salvage site and a boat configuration before sending the fleet out. Six boat types and five salvage sites give you choices to weigh against tides, weather, depth, fuel and hull condition. What comes back is weathered, and you restore it. The collection holds 60 artifacts, and you fund 20 harbor projects while working through 60 expedition contracts across five areas, from Home Harbor through Coral Reach, Stormwatch Isles and Sunken Passage to the Lighthouse Coast. Workshops, storage and museum facilities turn the harbor into a home for maritime history. A four-part tutorial gets you started. Plan at your own pace, pause when you need to think, and change the simulation speed. Three save slots let you try different approaches. Core play works offline. English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Free with optional rewarded ads and ads at breaks between expeditions.
