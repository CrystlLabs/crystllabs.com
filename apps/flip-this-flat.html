Flip This Flat

Android Volume Live

Buy a wreck, clear it out, furnish it and sell it on. One flat, three minutes.

Buy a wreck. Clear it out. Furnish it. Sell it for more than you paid. Flip This Flat is a renovation game about one apartment at a time. No timers to wait out, no energy bar, no queue. Three places on the market at once: a wreck is cheap to buy and dear to fix, a dated one is the other way round. Somebody left a lot behind, so tap it out of there and check what you find. Then drag furniture from a catalogue of hundreds of pieces, because a room filled too little looks abandoned and one filled too much looks like a warehouse. A young family wants bedrooms, an investor wants every inch working. Decorate for whoever is coming.
