Puff

Android Volume Store prep

Hold to puff up and float, let go to sink. Slip a tiny fish through the coral.

Puff is a one-finger swimming game about a round little clownfish on an endless reef. Hold your finger down and he puffs up like a balloon and floats up. Let go and he lets the air out and sinks. That is the whole control. Pink coral grows from the sea floor and hangs from above with a gap between, and each gap you slip through is a point. Bump the coral, the sand or the surface and the run is over. Shiny bubbles drift in the gaps. Each one is a coin, but it also makes Puff bigger for a few seconds, so the next gap feels tighter. Grab it or leave it. As you go the coral closes in, the current speeds up, some coral sways, and sleepy jellyfish float right in the gaps. Coins buy 9 cube pets to swim with, from a crab to a bee. Free with ads, nothing to buy, no account, plays offline.
