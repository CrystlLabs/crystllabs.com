Afterhours Cafe

Android Volume Store prep

Lay out the floor, write the menu, learn the regulars. A cafe that opens after dark.

Afterhours Cafe is a management sim about a cafe that does its business after dark. You place counters, tables and service stations on an open floor, then watch guests come in, wait, order and leave. Thirty recipes balance ingredients, freshness and price, so the menu is a set of trade-offs rather than a list. You hire baristas, keep an eye on team fatigue and set the opening hours. Eighteen regulars come back night after night, and their stories come out over repeat visits. Sixty contracts give each night a target, and research and equipment upgrades widen what the cafe can do. Pause whenever you need to think, change the simulation speed as the place gets busy, and try different plans across three save slots. Core play and progression work offline. Optional rewarded ads give a bonus, and interstitial ads appear at breaks between shifts. Plays in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.
