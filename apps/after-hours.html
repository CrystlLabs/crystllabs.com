After Hours

PC Volume Live

Stream from a fading arcade: play retro mini-games live, read your chat and grow a channel from three viewers.

Your first audience has three viewers, and one is probably your cousin. Build Eli's channel from the storage room of an aging neighborhood arcade and find out what it takes to keep a community coming back. Every episode is a live show: play a short arcade challenge while chat moments pop up, match the sound mix and pick the highlight that goes out. There are 30 episodes across three original arcade games, Brick Signal, Orbit Catch and Echo Grid, four show formats, six collaborators and 24 studio upgrades. Six story chapters each bring a choice, leading to three channel endings.
