Brambleguard

Android, PC Volume Live

Survive the waves, stack the upgrades, build a new knight every run.

A tiny guardian against a whole forest. Your attacks fire on their own, so your job is to move: dodge the waves, use the rocks and the marsh, and pick up experience crystals. Each level up offers a choice from nine upgrade paths, from blades and bows to frost and nature orbs, and the right pairs combine into something stronger. Short runs, three trails, no two builds alike.
