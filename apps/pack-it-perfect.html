Pack It Perfect

Android Volume Live

Rotate the camera, the books and the socks until the case is filled with no gaps.

Pack It Perfect turns books, cameras, clothes and travel essentials into shape puzzles. You pick an item, rotate it and find its place inside the case. The goal is to cover every square without overlapping another item or crossing the edge. Tap a packed item to take it back out and try a different arrangement. Every generated case has a complete packing solution, so a dead end means a different fit, not an impossible case. You travel through six packing themes, from weekend trips to moving day, with cases that get roomier as you go and a separate daily challenge. Place items by tapping or dragging, rotate pieces and undo recent moves. Two free hints per round each place an item for you and send any blockers back to the tray. Your placements and rotations are saved when you leave. Core puzzles work offline, with no account and no purchases. Optional rewarded videos give extra hints.
