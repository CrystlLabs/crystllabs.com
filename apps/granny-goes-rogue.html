Granny Goes Rogue

Android Volume Store prep

A retired gran sneaks past the guards, grabs the loot and gets home in time for tea.

Granny Goes Rogue is a cozy stealth puzzle. A retired gran has one last job: sneak through the museum, grab the loot and get out before a guard spots her. Nobody gets hurt. If she is seen, a guard walks her home and she tries again. Swipe to shuffle one tile at a time, because Granny does not run. Guards walk their rounds with their sight cones on show, cameras sweep the halls, and every room is a small timing puzzle. Drop biscuits to call a guard away for a few seconds, or roll a ball of yarn and nearby guards stop to watch it. There are 60 hand-checked levels across an ice-cream parlour, a souvenir shop, a condo lobby and three museum halls, and a bot has solved every one. Take all the loot unseen and on time for three stars, and spend your takings on 40 pieces of furniture for her living room. Free with optional ads. No purchases, no account.
