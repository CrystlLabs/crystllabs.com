Cube Zoo

Android Volume Live

Pair two cube animals, wait out the egg, and fill your grandmother's breeding notebook.

Your grandmother ran a small zoo on an island, and the sea took half of it. You inherit the pens that are left, the debt on them, and her breeding notebook: 24 species, most of the pages still blank. Pair two cube animals and wait for the egg, anywhere from one minute to thirty. The hatchling mixes coat color, brightness and body size from both parents, and up to three in ten keep the other parent's tail, wings or horns. Fifteen pairings hatch a third species instead. Shiny animals turn up about one time in forty and earn two and a half times as much. Animals keep banking coins while the app is closed, so a two-minute visit pays. Common animals you buy, everything rarer you breed, tier by tier. There are 38 goals and eight letters from your grandmother, and nothing in the zoo ever gets sick or dies. Offline, no account, no purchases. Free with ads.
