Inkline Panic

Android Volume Store prep

Your next step does not exist yet. Draw it, then watch the ink fade under your runner.

Inkline Panic is a runner where the ground ahead does not exist until you draw it. A little ink creature keeps moving across notebook pages full of gaps, and you sketch bridges and ramps with your finger before it gets there. Every stroke uses ink and fades after a few seconds, so a bridge drawn too early is gone by the time it is needed, and dashed lines warn you that one is about to disappear. Ink comes back while you are not drawing, which makes holding back part of the skill. Erasers cut through your lines, so you watch the route and make each stroke count. Collect stamps along the way and reach refill points to top up your supply. Doodle stars earned in play unlock ink color styles. Plays offline with no account, in English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Free, with occasional ads between runs and optional rewarded ads for extra stars.
