KPop Idol Trainee

Android Volume Live

Twelve months, nine trainees, seven spots. Every text you send decides who debuts.

You are the new trainee at a small agency, and the whole game is your phone. Nine trainees, seven spots, twelve months until the line-up is read out in practice room 3. Your trainer keeps a sheet and never says what is on it. The top-ranked trainee is loud, brilliant and texts you at 1am. The one who can actually hear you sing is ranked last. A senior from the fourth floor only answers the moon emoji, and your mum wants a call on Tuesdays. Messages land month by month and you pick every reply. No typing, no timers. Everyone remembers what you said, so leaning on one person costs you with the rest, and if the buzz climbs too high the fancafe posts it for two thousand strangers. Twelve chapters, nearly 800 messages, twelve endings and sixty moments to collect. The agency and everyone in it are invented. English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Free with ads, no account, plays offline.
