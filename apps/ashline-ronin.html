Ashline Ronin

Android, PC Volume Live

Read the attack, time the parry, answer with a counter. Anime sword duels.

Draw your blade as Rei, a ronin who wins fights by reading them. Every enemy attack is telegraphed in red, so a clean parry is skill, not luck. Strike, parry and dash while you manage stamina and heat, break a guard, then turn the opening into a combo. Build your ronin between duels and take on bosses that punish button mashing.
